Protected by design

The environment is not your production system.

We preserve what makes the work difficult while minimizing what leaves the source organization. Production credentials and unrestricted raw-system access are not the product.

Minimized data Transformed identity Isolated credentials Partner-approved release

Controls, infrastructure, agreements, and permitted uses are established for each project before protected information moves. This is a design requirement, not a statement of blanket certification.

For your own AI systems

Lower costs. Train for the work that matters.

We can evaluate whether open-source or open-weight models can deliver the required quality at lower cost—and whether custom training is justified.

01 / Cost optimization

Measure the tradeoff.

Benchmark open models against hosted APIs on quality, latency, control, and serving cost.

02 / Supervised fine-tuning

Teach stable behavior.

Use carefully curated examples to make domain-specific behavior more consistent.

03 / Custom RL

Optimize against outcomes.

Improve multi-step performance inside a purpose-built environment with measurable success.

We recommend custom training only when it offers a measurable advantage over prompting, retrieval, routing, or a hosted model.
