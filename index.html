Method

Speed is a system

Hard problems become tractable when you get close enough.

We begin with the real work: the systems, incentives, exceptions, and people that make a problem stubborn. Then we build the shortest path to something useful.

  1. 01

    Find the drag

    Look for consequential work still trapped between people, portals, spreadsheets, and phone calls.

  2. 02

    Touch the work

    Learn the process from inside it. Build against the world as it is, not the market category.

  3. 03

    Make speed compound

    Turn what we learn into systems that make every decision, workflow, and launch faster than the last.
